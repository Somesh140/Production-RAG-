# Savings Account Policy

## Eligibility
Resident individuals aged 18 or above may open a savings account. Minors aged 10 and above
may open an account operated by a natural guardian. Non-resident customers must use the
NRE or NRO variants, not the domestic savings product.

## Minimum Balance
The average monthly balance (AMB) requirement is INR 5,000 for metro and urban branches
and INR 2,000 for semi-urban and rural branches. Salary accounts and accounts flagged as
Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts (BSBDA) are exempt from any minimum balance.

## Non-Maintenance Charges
If the AMB falls below the threshold, a non-maintenance charge is levied at month end:
INR 300 plus applicable taxes for metro/urban, INR 150 for semi-urban/rural. The charge
is capped at 5% of the shortfall and is waived for the first three months after account
opening.

## Interest
Interest accrues daily on the end-of-day balance at 3.0% per annum up to INR 1,00,000 and
3.25% per annum above that, credited quarterly.

## Dormancy
An account with no customer-induced transaction for 24 months is classified as inactive
and for 24 further months as dormant. Reactivation requires fresh KYC and a branch visit.
