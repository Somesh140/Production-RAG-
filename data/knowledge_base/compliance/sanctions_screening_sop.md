# Sanctions Screening SOP

## Screening Points
Screen at onboarding, on every material data change, on each cross-border payment, and on
a full portfolio rescreen whenever a sanctions list is updated.

## Lists
United Nations consolidated list, the domestic list, and applicable OFAC and EU lists. The
lists are refreshed daily and a list version hash is recorded with each screening run.

## Handling a Hit
A potential match is frozen pending review. A level-1 analyst clears obvious false
positives within 4 hours using date of birth, nationality, and identifiers. Unresolved
hits go to level-2 and, if still unresolved, the payment is rejected and an STR is
considered.

## True Match
On a confirmed match the funds are frozen, no tipping-off occurs, and the matter is
reported to the regulator and the Financial Intelligence Unit the same day.
