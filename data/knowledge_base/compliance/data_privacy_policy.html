Data Privacy Policy

Lawful Basis and Notice

Personal data is collected for a specified purpose with notice to the customer. Processing

relies on consent, contract performance, legal obligation, or a legitimate interest that

does not override the customer's rights.

Customer Rights

Customers may request access to their data, correction of inaccurate data, and erasure

where retention is no longer required by law. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

Sharing

Data is shared with credit bureaus, regulators, and service providers under contract.

Cross-border transfer is only to jurisdictions with adequate protection or under approved

contractual safeguards.

Retention and Security

Data is retained only for the statutory period after the relationship ends. Access is

role-based, sensitive fields are encrypted at rest, and every access to a customer record

is logged.

Breach Notification

A personal data breach likely to cause harm is reported to the regulator within 72 hours

and to affected customers without undue delay.
