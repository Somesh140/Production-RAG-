Rewards Program Sheet

Earning

Earn 2 reward points per INR 150 spent on retail purchases and 6 points per INR 150 on

dining, travel, and online spends. Fuel, wallet loads, rent payments, and EMI conversions

do not earn points.

Redemption

Points may be redeemed against the statement as cashback at 1 point = INR 0.25, or in the

rewards catalogue at up to 1 point = INR 0.40. A minimum of 500 points is required to

redeem.

Expiry

Points expire 24 months after the month in which they were earned. Expiring points are

shown on the statement for the three cycles before expiry.

Milestones

Spend INR 4 lakh in a card anniversary year for 10,000 bonus points and a waiver of the

next year's annual fee.
