Auto Loan Product Sheet

What It Covers

New passenger cars and certified pre-owned cars up to 5 years old at the end of the loan

tenure.

Key Terms

  • Funding: up to 100% of ex-showroom price for new cars, 80% of valuation for used cars.
  • Tenure: 12 to 84 months for new, 12 to 60 months for used.
  • Rate: linked to the repo benchmark with a spread of 200 to 400 basis points by risk grade.
  • Processing fee: 0.5% of the loan amount, minimum INR 3,000, maximum INR 10,000.

Eligibility

Minimum age 21, maximum age 65 at maturity, minimum net monthly income INR 20,000, and a

bureau score of 720 or above.

Security

The vehicle is hypothecated to the bank and the bank's charge is noted in the registration

certificate. Comprehensive insurance with the bank as loss payee is mandatory for the

tenure.
