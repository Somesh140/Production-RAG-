# Loan Restructuring SOP

## When Restructuring Applies
Restructuring is considered for borrowers in genuine hardship - job loss, medical event,
business disruption - who are not wilful defaulters and whose account is standard or in
early delinquency (up to 89 days past due).

## Options
- Tenure extension of up to 24 months, keeping the EMI affordable within a 60% FOIR.
- A moratorium on principal of up to 6 months, with interest serviced monthly.
- One-time part settlement of overdue interest capitalised into the principal.

## Approval Matrix
Relationship manager recommends; credit officer approves up to INR 25 lakh; regional
credit head approves above that. Every restructuring is reported to the bureau with the
"restructured" flag.

## Post-Restructuring Monitoring
The account is monitored monthly for 12 months. A missed EMI in that window moves the
account straight to recovery without a further restructuring offer.
