# Branch Operations SOP

## Start of Day
Dual custodians open the vault and record cash in the joint custody register. Teller cash
limits are INR 2 lakh per counter; excess is remitted to the vault immediately.

## Cash Transactions
Any single cash deposit or withdrawal of INR 10 lakh or more requires branch manager
sight verification and a note of the purpose. PAN is mandatory for cash deposits of
INR 50,000 or more.

## Instruments
Demand drafts of INR 50,000 and above must be issued by transfer, not against cash.
Stale-dated instruments (older than three months) are returned unpaid.

## End of Day
Physical cash is tallied against the system balance. Any difference is recorded, escalated
to the regional office the same day if above INR 5,000, and provisioned.

## CCTV and Records
CCTV footage is retained for 180 days. Account opening records are retained for 10 years
after account closure.
